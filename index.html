Artwork in.

Fourteen days, four steps, zero surprises. You deal with one designer from first sketch to tracking number — never a ticket queue.

01

Design it

24H proofs

Send finished artwork or a sketch on a napkin. Our designers turn it into production-ready mockups within 24 hours.

02

Approve the proof

Unlimited revisions

Review every piece on your squad's real size range. Revise until it's exactly right — we don't print until you say go.

03

Print & cut

Full sublimation

Dye is bonded into the fiber under heat and pressure, then each garment is cut and sewn individually. The design can't crack, peel or fade.

04

Live tracking to your door. Reorders fly out in seven days — your artwork stays on file, forever.

(03) — Material spec

Fabric with
a job to do.

Nothing here is stock. Fabric is knitted, printed and cut for your run — which is why a reorder matches the first batch perfectly, five years later.

01

Re:cycle jacquard

145 GSM

82% recycled polyester / 18% elastane. Four-way stretch, dry in minutes, soft enough for a full double-header.

02

Full-bleed sublimation

6-COLOR

Ink becomes dye inside the fiber — no layers, no cracking, no peeling. Rated past 200 industrial washes.

03

Flatlock seams

0 CHAFE

Every seam locked flat against the skin. Tested over marathon distance, not just in a showroom.

04

Laser-cut ventilation

323 HOLES

Micro-perforated heat zones at the spine and underarms, mapped from sweat-panel testing on real athletes.

05

Graded blocks

7 SIZES / 2 BLOCKS

Men's and women's fit blocks from XS to XXL, tailored on request. Same artwork, cut for the body wearing it.
